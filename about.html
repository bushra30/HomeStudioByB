Introducing

HomestudiobyB

At HomeStudioByB, we see your home as more than just four walls—it’s where your memories are made, where life unfolds, and where every detail should reflect the things that matter most to you.Specializing in full home renovations and remodeling, we transform spaces into beautiful, functional environments that are both visually stunning and effortless to maintain. Whether it’s a kitchen revamp, a bathroom remodel, or an entire home transformation, we’re here to bring your vision to life.What sets us apart is our dedication to making the renovation process as smooth and stress-free as possible. We understand that home projects can feel overwhelming, so we’re with you every step of the way—listening, planning, and designing to ensure your dream space comes to life seamlessly. Our approach balances elegance with practicality, crafting spaces that not only look amazing but also fit effortlessly into your daily life. Let us help you create a home where you can truly live, relax, and enjoy for years to come.

Meet the Designer

Introducing

Bushra Hamidi

HomestudiobyB

As a Software Quality Engineer, I’ve spent years fine-tuning processes and solving complex problems, always with an eye for detail and precision. My commitment to quality has been the foundation of my career, and it naturally extends into my passion for Interior Design. It was during the renovation of my own home that I truly discovered this new love. The joy of selecting the right materials, experimenting with different styles, and making thoughtful design decisions became more than just a project; it was a transformative experience that showed me how much I enjoy creating spaces that not only look beautiful but also function flawlessly. I realized I could help others navigate the often-overwhelming renovation process with the same care and dedication.

At the heart of my design philosophy is the belief that a home should be both stunning and effortless to live in. A well-designed space shouldn’t require constant upkeep to maintain its appeal; it should feel polished and inviting every day with minimal effort. I focus on choosing durable, elegant materials and designing layouts that promote effortless flow. My goal is to achieve that perfect balance between beauty and practicality, whether it's a cozy family home or a sleek, modern kitchen.

Through HomeStudioByB, I work closely with my clients to bring their vision to life—without the stress or hassle. I’m here to guide you through every step, ensuring your home reflects your personality while also enhancing your daily life. Whether you’re seeking a serene retreat, a functional and vibrant kitchen, or a modern, minimalist space, I’m dedicated to creating designs that stand the test of time—and life—all while upholding the highest standards of quality.
